`/about` displays a short overview of PoroScout with links to useful resources. The response is only visible to you (ephemeral).

## What it shows

- A brief description of what PoroScout does.
- Links to the website, support server, terms of service, and the bot invite.
- A note about voting on top.gg.
- Which shard and cluster your interaction was handled by.

Two buttons are included: **Donate** (Ko-fi) and **Vote** (top.gg), plus a disabled button showing the current bot version commit hash.

## Usage

```text
/about
```

No options.
